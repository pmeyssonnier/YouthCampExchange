The Program

One summer. Two experiences.
Friends for life.

A Lions Youth Exchange is much more than a trip abroad. It is a cultural immersion built on two pillars — family life and camp life — where young people debate today's topics: environment, social media, mobility, and more.

1
🏠

Host family stay

Your journey starts in the home of a local Lions host family. Share their daily life, their table and their traditions — the fastest way to truly understand a culture from the inside.

2
🏕️

International camp

Then join an international camp with youth from all over the world: activities, excursions, service projects and debates on the questions that matter to your generation.

3
🤝

A worldwide network

You come home with more than memories: confidence, independence, and friends on every continent. Many participants stay in touch for decades — some for life.

How to apply

Five steps between you and the summer of your life. All documents and communication are in English — every form must be completed on a computer.

1

Find a sponsor club

Contact a Lions Club in your district willing to sponsor your application. Your club president will also sign your form.

2

Contact your district responsable

Reach out by phone or e-mail (see below) and download the application form specific to your district.

3

Complete the form & documents

Fill in the Excel form in English on a computer. Print page 4 as PDF and collect three digital signatures: you, your parent(s) and the club president.

4

Pay the administration fee

Transfer the €125 administration fee to the YCE account, mentioning your name, destination and district. Save the proof as PDF or JPEG.

5

Submit & get matched

Send the complete file to your district responsable, who verifies it and coordinates your placement with the Youth Exchange team.

📄 Required documents

  • Application form for your district (download below), completed in English on a computer — handwritten forms are rejected XLSX
  • Page 4 of the form, printed as PDF with three digital signatures: applicant, parent(s) and sponsoring club president PDF
  • Commitment to Reciprocity form, digitally signed — 📥 download PDF
  • Letter of introduction to your (still unknown) host family, in English: who you are and why you want to take part DOCX
  • Passport scan — or Belgian ID card scan for destinations within Europe PDF/JPG
  • Passport-style photo JPG
  • Proof of payment of the €125 administration fee PDF/JPG

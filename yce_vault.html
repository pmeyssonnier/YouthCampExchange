Data protection. The data controller is Lions Multiple District 112. Your application file is stored in a secure vault hosted in the European Union. Only you, your parent or guardian, your club president, your district coordinator and the national YCE team can open it, and every access is recorded. Your club president only receives a reduced copy, without health, religion or identity-document data. It is kept for 2 years after the end of the season, because the reciprocity commitment means your family hosts a young person the following year; it is then deleted automatically (90 days after a refusal). Health data (section III of the form) is processed only to organise a safe stay, with your explicit consent. Transfer abroad: if you are selected, the file is sent to the Lions youth exchange team of the host country, which may be outside the European Union; it is needed to organise your stay. To access, correct or delete your data, contact your district coordinator.
